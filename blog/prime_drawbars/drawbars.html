Prime Drawbars

An organ whose stops are the primes. Stop p sounds at p × 10 Hz: 2 is 20 Hz, 3 is 30 Hz, 5 is 50 Hz. No stop is an overtone of another, so the only thing they share is the 10 Hz they are all multiples of, a fundamental nobody plays. Switch to Pulse to slow the same chord down until it becomes a polyrhythm.
