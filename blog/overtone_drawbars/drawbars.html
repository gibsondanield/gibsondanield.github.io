Two notes, ten overtones each. Choose an interval, then switch between just intonation, 12-tone equal temperament, and equal temperament with every overtone moved onto the 12-TET grid. Roughness uses the Plomp–Levelt curve as parameterised by Sethares (Tuning, Timbre, Spectrum, Scale, 2005): every pair of sounding overtones adds min(a₁, a₂)·(e−3.51 s Δf − e−5.75 s Δf), s = 0.24 / (0.0207 fmin + 18.96). Just intervals are 5-limit.
