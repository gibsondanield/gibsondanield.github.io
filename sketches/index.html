Generative sketches

Processing (Java), 2013–15 · ported to p5.js in 2026

Interactive sketches from grad school. Most of them push every pixel through some small piece of math (XOR, trig, logs, distance) and use the mouse position as a parameter. Each one opens as its own page. Move the mouse around.

Pixel math

  • Dynamic triangle flowerXOR bitwise operations combined with sine waves.
  • Radial gradients (Feb 23)Distance-based gradients. The source has 30+ commented-out experiments, plus a println("monkeys") I can't explain.
  • Log patterns (Feb 25)Logarithmic colour mixing across the RGB channels.
  • ExponentsGeneralized p-norm circles. Mouse Y sets the exponent.
  • Square shadeXOR patterns in the blue channel only.
  • Multiplicative gradient (Feb 24)Static, no interaction.

Geometry and recursion

  • Recursive treeFractal branching, based on a Daniel Shiffman example. Mouse X sets the angle, Y the branch scaling.
  • Super circleRotating pentagonal symmetry. Left click rotates, right click clears, scroll shifts colours.

Newer experiments

  • Interference latticeSeveral oscillating sources interfering on a discrete phase grid.
  • Recursive decayRecursion at the pixel level: each pixel updates from its neighbours' state.

Every sketch does per-pixel work in loadPixels()/updatePixels(), so some are slow on weaker devices. The original .pde sources are kept alongside, e.g. Tree.pde.
